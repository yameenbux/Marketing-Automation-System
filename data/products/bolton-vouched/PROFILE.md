Per Markel, tradespeople give personal and company details, complete an evaluation for their trade and verify their identity. Pricing: Per Markel Direct (21 Oct 2025): 'MyBuilder has no membership fees'; a fee is charged when a homeowner shortlists the tradesperson, to access the customer's contact details. No amount is given. Not confirmed on mybuilder.com (403).. <sub>https://www.markeluk.com/articles/best-websites-for-tradespeople</sub>

Pricing: Its tradesperson help page describes a fixed lead plan on a 12-month term, priced by phone 'based on your trade and area', and an Opportunities Board where signing up is free and 'you only pay when you choose to unlock a lead'. No figures or currency are published. Its site says 'No commission rates' and 'Never pay for your leads from TrustATrader.com'; 'Memberships are limited per area so you get a fair share of leads.'. <sub>https://www.trustatrader.com/, https://www.trustatrader.com/join-us</sub>
