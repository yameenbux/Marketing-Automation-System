## Overview

- Tidemark is an iPhone weight tracker that focuses on your trend rather than the day-to-day swings on the scale.  
  <sub>`overview-trend-not-noise` — “**A weight tracker that shows the trend, not the noise.**”</sub>
- Your weigh-ins, your trend and your plan live on your phone and nowhere else.  
  <sub>`overview-on-your-phone` — “Weigh-ins, the trend and your plan, on your phone and nowhere else.”</sub>

## Target audience

- Tidemark is designed for people who are trying to lose weight and want a clearer read on their weigh-ins.  
  <sub>`audience-losing-weight` — “what it does for someone trying to lose weight”</sub>
- It also suits people who take a regular medication dose and want a simple record of when they took it.  
  <sub>`audience-medication-users` — “on a dose day, one tap marks the dose taken”</sub>
- It is for people who would rather keep their health data on their own device, with no account to create.  
  <sub>`audience-privacy-minded` — “No account, no server, and nothing leaves the phone unless you send it.”</sub>

## Pain points addressed

- A bathroom scale gives one number a day, and that number can swing with salt, water, sleep or a late meal.  
  <sub>`pain-scale-swings` — “that number swings by a kilo or more with salt, water, sleep and a late meal”</sub>
- Seeing the number go up on a bad day, even when you are doing everything right, is when many people give up.  
  <sub>`pain-giving-up` — “sees it go *up* on a Tuesday and decides it isn't working. That is the day most”</sub>

## Value proposition

- Tidemark reads your numbers properly: a trend one odd day can't drag far, an honest weekly rate and a plan to hold it against.  
  <sub>`value-read-numbers-properly` — “a trend that one odd day can't drag far, an honest weekly”</sub>
- The target line is a guide, not a verdict, and nothing in the app scolds you.  
  <sub>`value-guide-not-verdict` — “**The target line is a guide, not a verdict.** That sentence is the product.”</sub>

## Verified features

- The trend uses Holt's linear smoothing, which follows the direction you're heading rather than trailing behind like a plain moving average.  
  <sub>`feature-holt-smoothing` — “rather than trailing a week behind it the way a plain moving average does.”</sub>
- When a weigh-in jumps, the Trend tab explains why that is normal.  
  <sub>`feature-trend-explains` — “When a weigh-in jumps, the Trend tab explains why that's normal, and nothing”</sub>
- Logging opens on your last weight with minus and plus steppers, so most weigh-ins take a tap or two.  
  <sub>`feature-quick-logging` — “**Logging opens on your last weight**, with − and + steppers. Most weigh-ins”</sub>
- Reminders never show a number, and the dose reminder doesn't even name the medication.  
  <sub>`feature-discreet-reminders` — “The dose reminder doesn't even name the medication.”</sub>
- The medication log records what you took and never suggests a dose or a change of dose; Tidemark is a record, not medical advice.  
  <sub>`feature-medication-record` — “**Tidemark is a record, not medical advice.** The medication log never suggests”</sub>
- You can export a backup or CSV to wherever you choose, and backups can be locked with a password.  
  <sub>`feature-protected-backups` — “A file you choose where to put. Backups can be locked with a password”</sub>
- Every restore takes a snapshot first, and the snapshot is kept for 30 days in case you need it.  
  <sub>`feature-undo-and-snapshots` — “The snapshot taken before the restore is kept for 30 days”</sub>
