## Pain points addressed

- Booking by DM means late-night availability questions, and by the time she replies the client has often gone quiet and the slot stays empty.  
  <sub>`pain-dm-booking` — “By the time she answers, the client has gone quiet and the slot is still empty.”</sub>
- Marketplace booking sites show her client other technicians nearby; Booked In keeps the page hers alone.  
  <sub>`pain-marketplace-competitors` — “her photographs, her prices, her link, and no marketplace”</sub>
- Most booking platforms leave patch-test compliance to the technician, even though a missing dated record can put her insurance cover at risk.  
  <sub>`pain-patch-test-compliance` — “**Every booking platform treats this as her problem.**”</sub>
- The rebooking reminder a studio means to send often never gets sent.  
  <sub>`pain-rebooking-forgotten` — “it is simply the message a studio means to send and never does.”</sub>
