# Creative brief — Tidemark

**Campaign:** `tidemark-2026-10-21-749d0c`  **Objective:** awareness: introduce the product to its target audience with verified facts
**Status:** draft — not published. Needs human review of copy, visuals and narration.

## Audience (from verified facts)
- Tidemark is designed for people who are trying to lose weight and want a clearer read on their weigh-ins. `[audience-losing-weight]`
- It also suits people who take a regular medication dose and want a simple record of when they took it. `[audience-medication-users]`
- It is for people who would rather keep their health data on their own device, with no account to create. `[audience-privacy-minded]`

## Single-minded message
Tidemark is an iPhone weight tracker that focuses on your trend rather than the day-to-day swings on the scale.

## Call to action
https://tidemark.ysbdesigns.uk

## Must not say (known limitations — never imply these are done)
- Tidemark is built and tested but is not on the App Store yet; the Apple Developer enrolment is in review and the site says 'Coming soon to the App Store'.
- Buying and restoring Plus, iOS file encryption while locked and production hardening are written and unit-tested but not yet proven on a device.
- Apple Health sync, widgets and automatic backups are built but have not yet run on a phone, so they should be treated as in development.
- iCloud sync and an Apple Watch app are not built and must not be described as features.
- There is no sync between devices; a new phone gets your data from an iPhone backup or a Tidemark backup file.
- If you lose the phone without a backup, the data is gone, so the app reminds you to back up.
- The web build is the owner's private test tool, not a product, and is not offered to anyone.

## Visual direction
Brand background (42, 30, 69), accent (255, 107, 94), Inter Display headlines, real screenshots of the product's own
public site, word-by-word kinetic headlines, burned-in captions (most social video is watched muted).

## Formats
9:16 (Reels/Shorts/TikTok/Stories), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube/LinkedIn/web), plus stills and a 3-slide carousel.

## Success measure
Profile visits / link clicks / demo requests attributable to the post (UTM-tag the link when publishing).
