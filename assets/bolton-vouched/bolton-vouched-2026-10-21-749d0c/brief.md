# Creative brief — Bolton Vouched

**Campaign:** `bolton-vouched-2026-10-21-749d0c`  **Objective:** awareness: introduce the product to its target audience with verified facts
**Status:** draft — not published. Needs human review of copy, visuals and narration.

## Audience (from verified facts)
- For Bolton residents who need a trusted tradesperson, it is free to use with no account and no sign-up. `[audience-residents]`
- For local tradespeople, a basic listing on Bolton Vouched is free, for good. `[audience-traders]`

## Single-minded message
Bolton Vouched brings together local people, vouched for by Bolton.

## Call to action
https://boltonvouched.ysbdesigns.uk

## Must not say (known limitations — never imply these are done)
- The optional paid Pro tier is coming and not built; the dashboard only says it is coming soon, and there is no payment and no badge for sale.
- Pro is not built; it is planned at £10 a month or £100 a year, offered to a trader only after their 5th enquiry.
- Sign-in by text message is switched off, so residents sign in with Google or an email code instead.
- The policy pages are drafts pending legal review.
- Profile views are not recorded, so the trader dashboard does not show them.

## Visual direction
Brand background (110, 38, 22), accent (181, 134, 44), Inter Display headlines, real screenshots of the product's own
public site, word-by-word kinetic headlines, burned-in captions (most social video is watched muted).

## Formats
9:16 (Reels/Shorts/TikTok/Stories), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube/LinkedIn/web), plus stills and a 3-slide carousel.

## Success measure
Profile visits / link clicks / demo requests attributable to the post (UTM-tag the link when publishing).
