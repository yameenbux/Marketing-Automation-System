# Creative brief — Booked In

**Campaign:** `booked-in-2026-10-21-749d0c`  **Objective:** awareness: introduce the product to its target audience with verified facts
**Status:** draft — not published. Needs human review of copy, visuals and narration.

## Audience (from verified facts)
- It is made for people running a beauty business on their own. `[audience-solo-beauty]`
- Lash technicians who currently manage their diary through Instagram DMs are a core audience. `[audience-lash-technicians]`
- It serves both technicians who travel to clients and those who work from home, a rented chair or a shop. `[audience-mobile-and-fixed]`

## Single-minded message
Booked In is a booking page built for solo beauty professionals.

## Call to action
Find out more about Booked In.

## Must not say (known limitations — never imply these are done)
- Nothing has been provisioned yet: no email has been sent and no payment has been taken against a live provider.
- Deposits, custom domains, rescheduling and direct publishing to Instagram are not built and must not be promoted as features.
- Instagram publishing and deposits both depend on third-party approval processes, so neither should be promised.
- Booked In has not yet been tested on real Safari.

## Visual direction
Brand background (67, 25, 42), accent (196, 104, 120), Inter Display headlines, real screenshots of the product's own
public site, word-by-word kinetic headlines, burned-in captions (most social video is watched muted).

## Formats
9:16 (Reels/Shorts/TikTok/Stories), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube/LinkedIn/web), plus stills and a 3-slide carousel.

## Success measure
Profile visits / link clicks / demo requests attributable to the post (UTM-tag the link when publishing).
