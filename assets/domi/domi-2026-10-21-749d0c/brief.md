# Creative brief — Domi

**Campaign:** `domi-2026-10-21-749d0c`  **Objective:** awareness: introduce the product to its target audience with verified facts
**Status:** draft — not published. Needs human review of copy, visuals and narration.

## Audience (from verified facts)
- Domi is built for healthcare that comes to the patient's home, including eye care, hearing, foot care, dental, physio, blood tests, nursing and therapy assessments. `[audience-services]`
- Domiciliary eye care is the service Domi is built for today, and the demo is an eye-care practice. `[audience-eyecare]`

## Single-minded message
Domi is a diary for home-visit healthcare that refills itself when patients cancel.

## Call to action
https://domi.ysbdesigns.uk

## Must not say (known limitations — never imply these are done)
- Domi is a working demo and is not yet used by any practice.
- The demo is not for real patient data: it keeps everything in memory and holds invented patients only.
- Before real patients, Domi still needs a permanent UK database with tested backups, along with real accounts and compliance paperwork.
- Domi has no customers yet, so there are no testimonials or logos to show.
- Hearing care is ready to set up rather than built for, and Noah 4 integration is not built.
- PCSE notifications are still lodged by hand; sending them automatically is on the roadmap.
- Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have their own sight-test rules, which Domi doesn't model yet.
- NHS sight tests can't fill a same-day gap: PCSE needs a pre-visit notification at least 48 hours ahead.
- The hosted demo runs on a free plan that sleeps when idle and takes a short while to wake.

## Visual direction
Brand background (30, 77, 64), accent (224, 123, 79), Inter Display headlines, real screenshots of the product's own
public site, word-by-word kinetic headlines, burned-in captions (most social video is watched muted).

## Formats
9:16 (Reels/Shorts/TikTok/Stories), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube/LinkedIn/web), plus stills and a 3-slide carousel.

## Success measure
Profile visits / link clicks / demo requests attributable to the post (UTM-tag the link when publishing).
