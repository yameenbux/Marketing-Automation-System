# Creative brief — MasjidOne

**Campaign:** `masjidone-2026-10-21-2fad1a`  **Objective:** Introduce MasjidOne to UK masjid committees and drive demo requests
**Status:** draft — not published. Needs human review of copy, visuals and narration.

## Audience (from verified facts)
- Built in Bolton for masājid across the UK. `[audience-uk-masajid]`
- The website is designed for masjid committees who want to see what they would be buying and ask for a demo. `[audience-committees]`
- Teachers, parents and the masjid office each get their own separate view, so a parent only ever sees their own children. `[audience-roles]`

## Single-minded message
MasjidOne is one system that brings together a masjid's madrasah and its congregation.

## Call to action
https://masjidone.co.uk

## Must not say (known limitations — never imply these are done)
- Parent access and Hifz and sabaq progress are described as in development in one section, though a later section says both are built; confirm status before promoting them.
- The screen heartbeat is still tagged as in development and appears only in the demo.
- The demo request form's email service is written and tested but not yet deployed, so the form currently opens the visitor's own mail client.
- ICO registration and the data processing agreement are commitments promised before a masjid is invoiced, not yet completed facts.

## Visual direction
Brand background (12, 42, 33), accent (192, 164, 106), Inter Display headlines, real screenshots of the product's own
public site, word-by-word kinetic headlines, burned-in captions (most social video is watched muted).

## Formats
9:16 (Reels/Shorts/TikTok/Stories), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube/LinkedIn/web), plus stills and a 3-slide carousel.

## Success measure
Profile visits / link clicks / demo requests attributable to the post (UTM-tag the link when publishing).
